Warm Mocha

A warmer, near-black variant of Catppuccin Mocha, used across Ghostty, tmux and Vim. It is derived from the original Mocha colors by three rules. To tune them, change PARAMS in the script below, reload, and copy the exports into the configs.

Rules

All rules work in OKLCH: lightness L (0–1), chroma C (colorfulness) and hue H (degrees). Because OKLCH is perceptually uniform, keeping L keeps contrast unchanged.

1. Darken the surfaces

crust, mantle, base and surface0 are replaced by an evenly stepped ramp: L = + i · for i = 0…3, with chroma C = · L and Mocha's own surface hue (°). Each layer is a clearly visible step from its neighbor; chroma grows with lightness, as in Mocha, but is lower overall.

2. White-balance the neutrals

Mocha's grays are all tinted blue (H ≈ 275°). White balance corrects this like lowering a photo's color temperature: it multiplies the linear green and blue channels by fixed gains, then restores each color's original L. Because the gains are proportional, dark colors shift less than light ones.

The gains are g = (R / G)^s and b = (R / B)^s, where R, G, B are the linear channels of Mocha's text (#cdd6f4). At s = 1, text becomes exactly neutral gray; larger values turn grays warm. Currently s = , so g = and b = .

3. Pull the accents toward warm

White balance would desaturate blues, so accents get a hue rotation instead, with L and C unchanged: H′ = H + ° · sin(° − H). Each hue moves toward the warm pole along the shorter way around the color wheel. The pull is strongest for hues 90° from the pole and fades to zero at the pole itself and at its opposite. The bright ANSI colors from Catppuccin for Ghostty get the same treatment.
